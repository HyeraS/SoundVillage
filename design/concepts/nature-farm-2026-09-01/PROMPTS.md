Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished 2D pixel-art game map design proposal for Sound Village, an outdoor cozy NATURE village with a farm theme. This is a design concept image, not a diagram and not a sprite sheet.
Style reference: the three user-attached pixel farm screenshots and the existing Sound Village Nature, Animal and world-map screenshots visible in this conversation. Match the existing game's small pixel sprites, muted olive/sage grass, warm sandy paths, teal water, timber fences, cream UI and humble slate-roof cottages. Borrow the welcoming everyday countryside atmosphere of Animal Crossing but render exclusively as a classic orthographic top-down 2D RPG with south-facing sprite facades. Original buildings and characters, no copyrighted characters or logos.
Pixel discipline: consistent 16x16 source-tile vocabulary enlarged with nearest-neighbor, 1-source-pixel outlines (2 displayed pixels), coherent modest detailing, flat clustered 3-tone foliage, limited muted palette, no antialiasing or blurred textures, no tiny photographic noise, no glossy 3D, no isometric diamond ground, no tilted camera, no perspective vanishing point, no floating island diorama. Flat square-grid world without visible gridlines. Trees about 2 tiles wide; cottages about 5 tiles wide; a single black-haired traveler about one tile wide and 1.5 tall, wearing a simple cream shirt and brown pants. Character is small relative to village.
Framing: one landscape 4:3 image ideally 1536x1152, showing an entire approximately 48x36-tile village from above. The map fills the screen beneath a slim cream HUD at top, a soft irregular forest edge continues beyond the frame. It must feel like a connected inhabited outdoor village with several small cottages, farm plots and open grass, NOT an enclosed room, NO complete rectangular perimeter fence. Reserve generous explorable clear space; major paths 3 tiles wide, secondary paths 2 tiles wide, bridges at least 3 tiles wide. Every building door, crop plot and sound pickup connects to the path network. Every fence enclosure has visible open entrances. Rocks, tree trunks, buildings, crop beds, fences and water read clearly as obstacles that require walking around. Keep all pickup locations on dry reachable ground with a full tile of clearance. Empty grass is also traversable.
Gameplay: player explores then transcribes collected sounds. Show exactly six small floating pale-gold sound orbs with a tiny white abstract waveform inside, pixel highlight and small oval shadow; NOT musical-note pickups and NOT vegetables as pickups. Orbs are representative positions, not a claim of total sound count. Quiet environment, no combat, no livestock pens, no farming toolbar, no hearts, no currency. Nature rather than Animal village: wind, irrigation water, trees, crops and reeds carry the identity.
UI: slim elegant cream top bar with precise Korean labels: left "← 월드맵", then a leaf icon and "자연 마을", middle "소리 수집", right "구역 6 / 6". No numeric collection total. Small cream D-pad bottom left. Near traveler one small cream action pill "Enter · 소리 듣기". All other map objects unlabelled. UI is understated and does not cover important paths. No title banner, no presentation border, no inset image, no panel collage, no legend.
Research-game constraint: sound orb icons are visually identical and semantically neutral. Do not imply that an orb contains the sound of its nearby prop, do not add sound source labels or matching animal/music/weather symbols. Treat this whole-map display as an all-zones-open art preview, not a change to progression. All typography stays legible.
