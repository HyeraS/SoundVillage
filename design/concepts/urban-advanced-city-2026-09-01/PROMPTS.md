```text

Images 4 and 5 are the strongest references for the REQUIRED orthographic top-down gameplay view, landscape 4:3 framing, coarse pixel thickness, object scale, player scale, HUD geometry, path readability, and complete-map composition. Generate a brand-new original city map, not an edit and not a reproduction of any reference.

Primary request: one complete outdoor ADVANCED MODERN CITY district for a 2D sound-transcription exploration game. This must read as a developed contemporary or near-future metropolitan core—not a quaint village, old European town, suburb, medieval neighborhood, cozy cottage settlement, or rural market. Use mostly modern mid-rise and high-rise architecture with flat roofs, glass curtain walls, steel frames, clean concrete, rooftop HVAC, solar panels, digital transit elements, electric vehicles, and contemporary street furniture. A few trees and planters soften the city but do not dominate it.

View/style: polished orthographic top-down 2D pixel art, absolutely not isometric, not 3D, no perspective vanishing point, no cinematic street view. Use the same classic top-down game convention as the previous map concepts: rooftops plus short front-facing façades are visible, all structures align to the same flat map plane. Landscape 4:3 full gameplay screenshot. Chunky square pixels, broad flat clusters, crisp nearest-neighbor edges, restrained texture; visual equivalent of native 384×288 or 512×384 game art enlarged cleanly. Match the previous concepts' pixel size and camera distance exactly.

Composition/gameplay: clear south-center entrance with one small player just inside. Connected sidewalks, plazas, crosswalks, transit concourses, and pedestrian passages create a continuous loop through every district. Main walkways minimum two player sprites wide. Tall towers stay mainly along the north and outer edges so they establish skyline density without hiding the navigable center. Buildings, planters, lamps, barriers, parked vehicles, water, and transit structures read as clear collision masses. Doorways and station entrances face open walkable paving.

Sound collectibles: exactly SIX floating sound collectibles, evenly spread through the city. Each has a cool cyan-white circular glow with a simple white waveform and a tiny dark oval ground shadow. None on vehicle lanes, water, roofs, rails, behind barriers, or inside props. No dark square badges.

Color/mood: decisively cooler than the previous concepts. Steel blue, blue-gray concrete, charcoal asphalt, cyan glass, slate, cool white light, restrained violet accents. Small warm window lights may appear only as contrast; avoid dominant tan, beige, terracotta, orange, cottage wood, yellow awnings, and rustic brick.

Avoid: quaint village feeling, cottages, gabled terracotta roofs, bakery village, old clock pavilion, rustic market stalls, farm elements, excessive flowers, giant empty roads, highway interchange, cyberpunk clutter, post-apocalyptic grime, heavy neon magenta, photorealism, smooth vector art, thin anti-aliased lines, blurred pixels, isometric angle, diagonal camera, interior cutaway, multiple panels.
```
